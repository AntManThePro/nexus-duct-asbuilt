## Full description
Words draw the map. NEXUS turns the way techs actually talk — "up 8 feet, 90 right, 22 feet with a door, slant 45 up 6 into the hinged fan" —
into a to-scale as-built schematic of a commercial kitchen exhaust duct, then audits it against NFPA 96 on the spot.

• **Site & AHJ dossier** – facility, jurisdiction, duty class, fan type, hood type & size, collars, appliances under the canopy
• **Trade-slang parser** – offline fuzzy engine that understands typos, word numbers and shop slang; mic dictation where available
• **CAD + redline canvas** – 1 ft grid, animated flow hood → fan, dimension tags, door markers, pinch-zoom, drag nodes, component library, stylus redlines
• **NFPA 96 kernel** – horizontal access spacing (12 ft NFPA / 20 ft IMC), access at every turn, riser access, hinged upblast fan, in-line & sidewall rules, hood coverage, duty vs appliance class, §4.6 drawing-packet flags, compliance score and Table 12.4 inspection cadence
• **Packet & export** – owner-binder sheet (print / PDF), PNG with title block, JSON project files, share sheet, record ID & hash

100 % offline. No accounts, no tracking. Built by hood cleaners and mechanical techs for AHJ submittals, permit as-built gaps and service handoffs.
