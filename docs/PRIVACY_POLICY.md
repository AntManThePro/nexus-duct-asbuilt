# NEXUS Duct As-Built — Privacy Policy

_Effective: 2026-01-01_

NEXUS Duct As-Built ("the App") is an offline field tool for documenting commercial kitchen exhaust duct systems.

## Data we collect
**None.** The App has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and makes no network requests after installation.

## Data stored on your device
Project data you enter (site names, technician name, hood/appliance/duct geometry, redline markup, notes) is saved only in the App's
local storage on your device so your work survives app restarts. You can erase it at any time with **Reset** inside the App or by
uninstalling the App.

## Sharing
When you tap **Share**, **Save PNG** or **Save JSON**, the App hands the file to the operating system share sheet or file picker. Where that
file goes is entirely under your control; the App never transmits it anywhere itself.

## Microphone
Voice dictation (where supported) uses the operating system / browser speech recognizer. The App only receives the resulting text and does
not record or store audio. On iOS/Android you will be asked for microphone permission the first time you use the mic button; declining
simply disables dictation.

## Children
The App is a professional trade tool and is not directed at children under 13.
